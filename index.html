Keep a dated log on your own device.

Marginlog records each incident with the time from your device, stores a fingerprint of each entry with the one before it, shows patterns, and builds a report you can copy or print. A matching fingerprint is not proof of what happened or when, and the app is not legal advice. No account.

How it works

  1. Pick the kind of problem and name the case.
  2. Log incidents in under 20 seconds, or time one live.
  3. Hand over the report when you need it.

What is inside

  • Fingerprinted entries

    Each entry stores a fingerprint that includes the previous one. If stored data no longer matches, the report says so. That does not prove the words are true, and someone who calculates new fingerprints can still rewrite the log.

  • Happening-now timer

    Tap once while it is happening. Duration and start time fill in for you.

  • Pattern finder

    Weekday-by-hour heat map, 8-week trend, quiet-hour breaches, longest calm stretch.

  • Record detail

    Each entry gets a score for blank fields: length, duration, witness, photo, timing and a tag. It is a reminder, not a judgment of evidence.

  • Escalation prompts

    Talk, written notice, complaint, mediation, or a further step. These are suggestions from rules in the app, not a legal assessment. Dates you set can be marked overdue.

  • Report in one tap

    Copy, print to PDF, Markdown or CSV, with photos and a seal check at the bottom.

  • Draft a written notice

    A plain, factual letter built from your log, with a reply deadline. Edit it, then send it your way.

  • Deadlines in your calendar

    Export reply deadlines as a calendar file so an ignored letter does not slip by.

  • Ready to hand over?

    A short checklist tells you what would make the report stronger before you send it.

Honest answers

Will this hold up as evidence?

It is a personal log that can help you remember what you wrote down. Whether anyone accepts it is up to them. Marginlog does not say that it will. It is not legal advice.

What does the fingerprint show?

If stored entries are changed and the fingerprints are not recalculated, the report says the chain does not match. It does not prove the content, the time, or that someone cannot rewrite the log and calculate new fingerprints. It is not a notarised timestamp.

Where is my data?

Cases and photos stay in this browser. A license key can be sent to this site’s license check and, when that check is on, to Lemon Squeezy. Optional AI sends counts and dates only if you press the button. The privacy note has the list. Clearing site data deletes the local copy.
